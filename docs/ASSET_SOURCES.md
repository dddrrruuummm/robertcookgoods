# Asset sources — Robert Cook Goods

## Inherited KKANG product illustrations

The user requested reuse of the existing KKANG product images for Robert Andrew Cook's product-resale website. Twelve product assets were copied from the local `KKANG-Trading/public/images/` directory into this project's `public/products/` directory. The source KKANG project remains unchanged.

All twelve source product illustrations are 1122 × 1402 pixels. Their filenames are:

- `seat-cushion.png`
- `neck-pillow.png`
- `footrest.png`
- `monitor-light.png`
- `desk-tray.png`
- `acoustic-screen.png`
- `humidifier.png`
- `kettle.png`
- `magnetic-cable-rail.png`
- `felt-laptop-stand.png`
- `utility-caddy.png`
- `air-purifier.png`

The inherited source's `PRODUCT.md` identifies these as existing illustrative product assets, not approved inventory. No per-image generation log, approved SKU mapping, current stock information, manufacturer authorization, final product specifications, or reseller-specific image license is recorded in the source project. They must not be described as verified product photography or evidence of stock. Reuse here follows the user's direction; the site does not claim official KKANG authorization.

Product names are preserved from the inherited KKANG catalog. New descriptions explain intended settings and inquiry topics without adding performance, health, price, stock, certification, or delivery claims. Customers are directed to confirm details with Robert before ordering.

## Inherited editorial photography

Source and license metadata for the KKANG project's Pexels photographs has been copied to `docs/inherited-photo-sources.json`. That metadata includes the original source page, download URL, Pexels license page, retrieval date, and the original editorial-use limitation for each photograph.

These are real stock photographs of category and application scenes. They do not establish KKANG-owned products, facilities, staff, current inventory, or completed projects. If used in this website, they serve only as lifestyle or editorial atmosphere and must not be assigned to a specific catalog product as if they show its exact SKU.

Particular exclusions for product matching: the inherited `gallery/kettle.webp` depicts a visibly branded SMEG kettle, `gallery/desk-light.webp` depicts a table lamp rather than the monitor light, and `gallery/laptop-setup.webp` does not show the catalog's folded felt stand. None should replace the corresponding product illustration.

## Local files and delivery

Images are served from local project files. No client documents or original source images were uploaded to an external service during this asset audit. Keep this source record and the inherited photo metadata with the project so final image approval can be tracked before publication.
